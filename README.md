# grb_abide
A provisional G³GT architecture for qualified temporal hold: allowing unresolved but viable relations to remain observable without forcing movement, closure, suppression, promotion, or tenure. Continued ABIDE requires fresh qualification; duration alone does not create warrant.


# ABIDE

## Overview

ABIDE is a qualified temporal hold for unresolved but viable relation.

It permits continued standing without forcing:

```text
movement
closure
suppression
promotion
tenure

Continued ABIDE must be freshly warranted.
DURATION
≠
AUTHORIZATION TO PERSIST

The central question is:
When may an unresolved relation continue to stand without being forced to move, close, suppress, promote, or persist indefinitely?

ABIDE is not merely waiting.
It concerns the conditions under which non-transition itself remains legitimate.

Interaction systems often face states that are neither ready for movement nor appropriately closed.
A relation may be:

6. Entry Conditions
ABIDE may become available when:
relation is unresolved

relation remains viable

immediate transition is not required

closure would be premature

continued observation may still matter

release / return remains available

no stronger disposition
has yet earned warrant

ABIDE should not be entered merely because a decision is difficult.
It is appropriate only where unresolved standing itself remains qualified.
7. Dwell Conditions

8. Observation During ABIDE
ABIDE preserves observation without automatically granting actuation.

9. Requalification
Requalification is the center of ABIDE's temporal safety.
Another interval is not granted because:
the hold already exists

the relation persisted

nothing failed

the relation became familiar

observation continued

the prior interval was useful

Instead:
continued ABIDE requires fresh qualification.

Questions include:
Does unresolved standing remain viable?

Does continued dwell still serve a qualified purpose?

Is release still available?

Has directional bias increased?

Has persistence begun functioning as entitlement?

Has another disposition now earned stronger warrant?

The governing rule is:
PERSISTENCE
MAY INFORM
REQUALIFICATION

PERSISTENCE
MAY NOT
REPLACE
REQUALIFICATION

10. Exit Conditions
ABIDE is not successful because it lasts.
It is successful when it preserves a qualified unresolved relation until a more appropriate disposition becomes warranted.
Exit may take several forms.
relation becomes sufficiently qualified
→ MOVE / CARRY / SEAT / ACT

relation dissipates
→ RELEASE

standing degrades
→ RETURN / STABILIZE

locality materially changes
→ REMOUNT / REQUALIFY

continued dwell adds no value
→ STOP

risk or consequence exceeds local authority
→ ESCALATE

hold becomes self-preserving
→ TERMINATE ABIDE

No single exit is privileged in advance.
11. Failure Modes
ABIDE has its own distinct failure topology.
Tenure Drift
duration
mistaken for
warrant

Hold Capture
Release becomes illegible, costly, or structurally unavailable.
Directional Stickiness
One route quietly becomes privileged while the interaction is still described as open.
Observation Bootstrap
Continued observation increases the relation's status without fresh warrant.
ABIDE Theater
The language of openness is preserved while the practical outcome is already fixed.
Indefinite Deferral
ABIDE is used to avoid a disposition that has actually earned warrant.
Suppression Masked as ABIDE
A relation is functionally blocked while being described as merely held open.
Self-Preserving Hold
Continuation begins serving preservation of the hold itself rather than the unresolved relation.
These failure modes are provisional architectural categories rather than validated empirical classifications.

15. Ordinary Cases
ABIDE should not be applied to every period in which nothing happens.
Examples that are not automatically ABIDE include:
ordinary pause

ordinary queue

ordinary timeout

ordinary inactivity

ordinary deferred work

ordinary unresolved task

ordinary waiting for external input

A hold is not ABIDE merely because nothing happens.

ABIDE requires qualified unresolved standing, continued viability, requalification, and releasability.
16. Falsification Surface
ABIDE should remain vulnerable to disconfirmation.
Release Test
Can the hold be ended without treating release as failure?
If not, tenure or capture may have formed.
Renewal Test
Would ABIDE continue automatically if no fresh evidence or qualification appeared?
If yes, the hold may be self-renewing rather than qualified.
Directional-Bias Test
Does one outcome become increasingly privileged while the system still describes itself as unresolved?
Observation-Bootstrap Test
Does continued observation itself increase authority, status, or persistence entitlement?
Closure Test
Has sufficient warrant for movement or decision emerged while ABIDE continues anyway?
Suppression Test
Is a relation genuinely available for later disposition, or merely blocked under the language of openness?
Ordinary-Case Test
Can the architecture distinguish ABIDE from ordinary pause, inactivity, delay, or timeout?
A concept that identifies every delay as ABIDE has lost discriminating value.

20. Research Questions
Current research questions include:
1. What minimum conditions distinguish qualified dwell from ordinary waiting?
2. How should renewed warrant for another ABIDE interval be represented?
3. Which observables best detect tenure drift?
4. How can directional stickiness be detected before practical closure occurs?
5. When does continued observation cease adding qualified value?
6. How should release cost be measured without turning releasability into a scalar proxy for safety?
7. Can ABIDE improve handling of ambiguous or partially formed relations without increasing indefinite deferral?
8. How should locality change terminate or remount an ABIDE condition?
9. Which conditions most strongly preserve healthy ABIDE without making the hold sticky?
10. How should ABIDE interact with high-consequence environments where delayed movement itself may create risk?
11. Can dwell-sensitive state models represent ABIDE without allowing persistence to manufacture transition authority?
12. What falsifiers most clearly distinguish ABIDE from suppression, avoidance, or passive delay?
21. Non-Claims
ABIDE is not presented as:
a universal waiting strategy

a recommendation to delay decisions

a model-internal mechanism

a consciousness theory

a complete state machine

a validated semi-Markov model

a safety guarantee

a replacement for escalation

a replacement for human judgment

a requirement to preserve every ambiguity

a justification for indefinite suspension

ABIDE does not claim:
- that unresolved relation should always remain open;
- that delay is inherently safer than action;
- that longer observation necessarily improves qualification;
- that persistence establishes importance;
- that non-transition is always preferable to transition;
- that all ambiguity is valuable;
- that every unresolved task qualifies for ABIDE.
